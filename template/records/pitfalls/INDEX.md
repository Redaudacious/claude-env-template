# The project's pitfalls

Each pitfall is a file, dated in its name: `YYYY-MM-DD-short-subject.md`.
Entries **are not rewritten** — they describe what was true on the day they were
caught. Only the table below is rewritten.

What goes in here: anything that cost time and whose **method of finding is reapplied**.
The result belongs to the project; the method does not, and the method is written down.

The format of an entry is in the `documentation` skill, at `references/details.md`.
